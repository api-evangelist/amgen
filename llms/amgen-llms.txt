# Amgen

> Amgen is a biotechnology company that discovers, develops, manufactures and delivers human
> therapeutics. Founded in 1980, it uses living cells to make biologic medicines for
> cardiovascular and metabolic disease, oncology, inflammation, rare disease, bone health and
> obesity, and markets biosimilars, operating in approximately 100 countries.
